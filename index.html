Katherine Claye

Original Paintings & Works on Paper

Katherine Claye (b. 1997) is an artist currently based in Monroe, Louisiana. Originally from Alabama, she earned her Bachelor of Fine Arts in Studio Art from Auburn University in 2020 and has continued developing her practice through painting, drawing, and commissioned work.

Working primarily in acrylic and watercolor, Katherine’s work is expressive and ever-evolving, often combining loose brushwork, texture, color, and drawn elements. She enjoys exploring a variety of subjects and materials rather than limiting herself to a single style. Her work is inspired by nature, travel, people, interiors, music, and the everyday details that make a place or moment feel meaningful.

Art has also been a part of Katherine’s life through her family. Her grandmother, a landscape painter, has been a lasting source of inspiration and helped shape her appreciation for painting and the natural world.

Through social media, Katherine shares finished pieces alongside glimpses into her creative process and the continued evolution of her work.

When she isn’t painting, Katherine can usually be found trying a new recipe, spending time outdoors, rearranging something in her home, or hanging out with her cat, Maisey.
